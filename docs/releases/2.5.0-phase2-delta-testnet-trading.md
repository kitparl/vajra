# Phase 2 slice 5: Delta testnet trading and reconciliation

## Summary
With `TRADING_MODE=DELTA_TESTNET`, core-service now places and tracks real orders on the Delta Exchange India **testnet**. A 60 s reconciler keeps our records in line with Delta: it resolves `UNKNOWN` orders, sweeps stuck orders, records missing fills and compares positions, and it never overwrites our records. The kill-switch now works on a real venue. Paper remains the default, and live trading stays off. This slice completes Phase 2. It is an enhancement, so the bump is minor.

## Changes
- **Signed Delta client (`DeltaPrivateClient`):**
  - HMAC-SHA256 signing and the testnet base URL constant only.
  - Rate-limit guard. Cancels are never blocked, because Delta does not rate-limit them.
  - Error codes from every Delta error body shape. 5xx, I/O and unreadable bodies are retryable.
  - Credentials are trimmed and masked. Secrets never appear in logs, exceptions or `toString`.
- **Startup check (DELTA_TESTNET mode only):**
  - Startup fails on missing or malformed keys, `invalid_api_key` and clock skew (`SignatureExpired`).
  - Any other failure is a WARN only.
- **`DeltaTradingVenue`:**
  - Places market and limit orders with our 32-char `client_order_id`.
  - A market order polls up to 3 s for its final state.
  - A definite reject before Delta accepts the order becomes `REJECTED <code>`.
  - After Delta accepts an order, no failure can turn it into REJECTED. The order stays `ACKED` and the checker resolves it.
  - Fills are merged per order, and their total size is checked against Delta. Partial fills are refused loudly; risk caps orders at 1 contract, so they cannot occur yet.
- **`OpenOrderChecker`** (replaces `PaperMatcher`):
  - One 1 s job for every venue. Delta orders are checked every 2 s.
  - Covers `CANCEL_REQUESTED` orders, and re-sends a failed venue cancel.
- **`Reconciler`** (every 60 s, first run 10 s after start):
  - Stuck NEW/APPROVED orders are closed as not sent. Orders stuck in SENT for 30 s go to UNKNOWN.
  - UNKNOWN orders are resolved by `client_order_id`. One not found for 60 s becomes `NOT_FOUND_AT_VENUE`. An order is never re-sent.
  - Missing fills are recorded only for orders we know, exactly once.
  - Position and entry-price mismatches are logged at ERROR and never written.
- **Kill-switch on a real venue:**
  - Flattens on the position's own venue, even when the instrument has trading disabled.
  - `flattened` is true only when no order is in a non-terminal state and every position is 0.
  - The halt is re-checked in the same transaction as `APPROVED → SENT`.
  - Re-cancels `CANCEL_REQUESTED` orders.
  - A flatten rejected for `RATE_LIMITED` gives `flattened=false`.
- **Fail-closed by structure:** only a kill-switch reduce-only order may target a venue explicitly.
- **Reduce-only on a real venue:** a Delta fill is a fact. It is recorded, and a local disagreement is logged at ERROR. Paper still refuses the fill and cancels.
- **Compose:**
  - core-service gets `TRADING_MODE` (default `PAPER`), `DELTA_TESTNET_API_KEY` and `DELTA_TESTNET_API_SECRET`.
  - `.env.example` gets empty placeholders.
  - The Kafka `mem_limit` goes from 512m to 768m (measured reason below).
- **Docs:**
  - knowledge `0027-delta-testnet-trading-and-reconciliation.md`
  - Hinglish note `08-order-lifecycle/03-reconciliation-aur-unknown-order.md`
  - closed follow-ups in 0024 and 0026 marked `Superseded by: 0027`
  - the Kafka limit in the Hinglish devops note

## DB migrations
None.

## Contract changes
None to Protobuf or Kafka. `GET /api/v1/orders/{id}` behaviour is unchanged. Orders on `DELTA_TESTNET` now carry Delta's numeric order id as `venueOrderId`.

## Breaking changes
- **`TRADING_MODE=DELTA_TESTNET`** now really trades on the testnet. It needs `DELTA_TESTNET_API_KEY` and `DELTA_TESTNET_API_SECRET` in the git-ignored `.env`, and the server IP must be whitelisted on the Delta demo account. Without valid keys, core-service will not start in that mode. PAPER mode is unaffected.
- **Switching back to PAPER:** do it only when the Delta position is flat and no Delta orders are open. Otherwise the reconciler logs an ERROR every 60 s and kill reports `flattened=false`.
- **Kafka restart:** the Kafka container is recreated by the mem_limit change. Data stays in the `kafkadata` volume.

## Verification
- **Builds and checks:**
  - `cd services && mvn -B verify`: BUILD SUCCESS. core-service has 153 unit and 112 IT tests; risk-service 16 + 30; gateway-bff 0 + 4; contracts 3; common 3.
  - `pytest`: 21 passed. ruff, `buf lint`, `role-isolation.sh` and `compose config` are all OK.
  - Delta REST is tested against recorded fixtures with fake keys. There is no network access in CI.
- **Process:**
  - Each of the 5 tasks was implemented by its own agent and reviewed; the money-path tasks were reviewed by the most capable model.
  - The reviews found and fixed, each with a failing test first:
    - A credential with a control character could leak through a JDK exception.
    - An order Delta had accepted could be recorded as REJECTED.
    - Cancels were blocked during a rate-limit pause.
    - Partial-fill and fill-visibility gaps.
    - A weak race test.
    - A reduce-only fill refused and then cancelled forever on a real venue.
  - The final whole-slice review found that a failed venue cancel was never retried. It is fixed.
- **Testnet demo** (Delta India demo account, 1 contract each):
  - Manual MARKET BUY then SELL: both FILLED. Fill price and fee equal Delta's values, and positions matched (reconciler `mismatches=0`).
  - Heartbeat signals at a 60 s interval: FILLED on testnet.
  - Connection cut mid-order: SENT → (30 s) UNKNOWN → RECONCILED → FILLED, with exactly 1 fill, about 75 s after reconnect.
  - Kill-switch with a position of 1: `flattened=true`, and the Delta position is 0.
  - After the demo: back to PAPER, both positions flat, trading resumed. No secret was found in any log, repo file or scratch file.
- **Memory:**
  - core-service: 181 / 384 MiB.
  - Kafka: the final check first failed at 480 / 512 MiB (93%; 426 MiB anon JVM memory; the limit was hit 51 times, with no OOM kill). After raising the limit: 367 / 768 MiB (47%).
  - Stack total: 1018 MiB.

## Known follow-ups
All are listed in knowledge doc 0027.
- **No private WebSocket:** REST polling plus reconciliation is used instead.
- **Partial fills:** per-fill rows and `PARTIALLY_FILLED` handling are needed before raising the 1-contract limit.
- **Alerts:** mismatches are ERROR logs until the Phase 5 notifier.
- **Halt race:** a small race remains between the SENT commit and the venue call. It shows up as `flattened=false`; press kill again.
- **Startup check:** it is warn-only for 401/403 codes not listed above (for example IP not whitelisted). Orders are then rejected, so it fails safe.
- **Position comparison:** it covers trading-enabled instruments only.
- **Still open from 0026:** a maximum signal age.

## Rollback notes
- **Quick:** set `TRADING_MODE=PAPER` (the default) and recreate core-service. First make sure the Delta position is flat and no Delta orders are open.
- **Full:** redeploy the 2.4.0 images and compose. There are no DB changes to undo.
- **Kafka limit:** setting it back to 512m is possible but not advised, because the measured usage is close to that limit.
